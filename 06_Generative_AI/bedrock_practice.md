Parameters in Bedrock
Parameters control AI model behavior:

Length

Maximum number of tokens (words) in the response
Temperature (0.0–1.0)

Controls creativity and randomness:
Low (0.0–0.3): Factual, consistent (math, code)
Medium (0.4–0.7): Balanced (conversations, explanations)
High (0.8–1.0): Creative, varied (writing, brainstorming)
Top-p (0.0–1.0)

Controls word choice diversity:
Low (0.1–0.5): Focused, precise words
High (0.9–1.0): More diverse word options
